## What I'm Working On
### Photonic Attention Accelerators: Softmax Noise-Budget Analysis

**Research | Transformers | Photonic Computing**

Researching the numerical reliability of softmax computations in photonic attention accelerators.

**Paper:** *Softmax Degradation in Photonic Attention Accelerators: A Noise-Budget Analysis*

**Status:** Under review at IEEE Photonics Journal.

- Investigating the effects of photonic noise on softmax computations.
- Developing a theoretical framework for photon-budget requirements.
- Deriving a KL-divergence bound to characterize computational degradation.
- Evaluating theoretical results using analytical calculations and Monte Carlo simulations.
- Studying the implications for transformer attention architectures.

**Tech:** Python, Numerical Analysis, Probability, Information Theory, Monte Carlo Simulation
